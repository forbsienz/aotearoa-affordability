# Narrative tone rules

Aotearoa Affordability builds short comparison sentences from **deterministic templates** in `narrative/tone.py`.

## Voice

| Avoid | Prefer |
| --- | --- |
| "You cannot afford rent here" | "Typical new tenancy rent is about $X/week" |
| "Wages are failing" | "Workplace median earnings moved +Y% year on year" |
| "Suburb inflation is Z%" | "Food prices in your CPI region were +Z% year on year" |

## Hard rules

- Never invent suburb-level CPI or HLPI.
- Label LEED as **workplace** earnings.
- Label bond rent as **new tenancies**.
- Mortgage figures are **illustrative** only.
- Prefer "tends to" / "about" over absolute claims when confidence is medium or low.
